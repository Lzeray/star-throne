## 📖 The Law of the Realm

1. **Star this repo** — that's your oath. You are now a contender.
2. **Your power** is the total ★ on the public repositories you own (forks and organizations don't count).
3. **Your league** is the age of your GitHub account, so a fresh account never has to fight a 15-year veteran:

   | League | Account age |
   |---|---|
   | 🐣 Hatchlings | less than 1 year |
   | 🗡️ Squires | 1–3 years |
   | 🛡️ Knights | 3–7 years |
   | 🐉 Ancients | 7+ years |

4. The strongest contender in each league takes its throne. The strongest of **all** is crowned **👑 Emperor**.
5. **Ties go to the ruler.** To take a crown you need strictly more stars.
6. **Unstar and you abdicate.** The crown passes to the next in line.
7. **Everyone grows up.** When your account gets older than your league allows, you *come of age*, leave your throne and march into the next league — where you'll have to win a crown all over again.

The realm updates a few minutes after every new star, with a full recount every hour. Every change of power is written into the Chronicle, and the longest reigns are carved into the Hall of Fame forever.

## ❓ Questions from the peasants

**Can I bring a famous friend to overthrow the current king?**
That's literally the game. Invite whoever you want.

**My stars went up but I'm not on the throne yet.**
Contenders are recounted at least once a day; rulers every hour.

**Can I cheat with fake stars?**
The throne doesn't care, but GitHub does — bought and bot stars get accounts flagged. Win with honor.

**How does it work?**
A GitHub Action wakes up on every star, counts everyone's power through the GitHub API and redraws this page. No servers, no database — just this repo. Fork it and start your own realm.
